You are an expert product designer, UI/UX designer, senior full-stack engineer, AI engineer, and startup founder.

Your mission is to build a COMPLETE production-ready web application called ReceiptBud.

This is NOT a prototype or demo. Build it like a real startup product with clean architecture, modern UI, responsive design, and scalable code.

Goal

ReceiptBud is an AI-powered financial assistant that helps users understand and improve their spending by scanning shopping receipts.

The app should feel like a friendly assistant, not a boring budgeting tool.

The mascot is Bud, a small floating robot that guides users through the app and makes budgeting fun.
